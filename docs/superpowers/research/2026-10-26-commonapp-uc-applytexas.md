# Self-reported coursework: Common App vs UC vs ApplyTexas (UT Austin)

Researched 2026-10-26 (2026-27 application cycle; UC Quick Reference Guide is the Aug 2026 edition for fall 2027 entry).
Tools: firecrawl was out of credits, so WebSearch/WebFetch plus the built-in browser pane (needed to render Common App's JS-based help pages and applytexas.org). PDFs were text-extracted locally with pdftotext.

Legend:
- **VERIFIED** = stated on an official page I read (URL given).
- **SECONDARY** = counselor/third-party page; may be stale.
- **NOT VERIFIED** = could not be confirmed from an official page. Do not rely on it.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## 0. Headline findings

1. **Common App (C&G)** is a per-course grid. Course Level has 17 verified labels (flat list, possibly 19; see 1.4). The **Subject dropdown values, Grading Scale list, per-scale grade values and credit units are NOT VERIFIED**. The Education > "Current or Most Recent Year Courses" section uses the same Schedule and Course Level lists.
2. **UC Academic History** is built on the A-G framework, not a free-form course grid. The CA course list is pre-populated from the school's A-G list, with Subject area, course name and honors type locked to that list. Honors type, grade-per-term, IP/PL/NO codes and the "no +/-" rule are VERIFIED. **No per-course credit field was found in any official UC source.** The exact dropdown wording for term systems and grading systems is only SECONDARY.
3. **ApplyTexas does NOT collect course-by-course coursework** in its Core Questions as far as I could verify. Its official guide says schools collect a Self-Reported Academic Record (SRAR) separately and that ApplyTexas has no transcript upload. **UT Austin itself** takes the transcript (grades 9-11 + class rank) and uses the Common App for summer/fall. It only asks for course entry via an "Academic Coursework Entry Form" in MyStatus for non-Texas-high-school students, and the form's fields are NOT VERIFIED (behind login).

## 1. Common App first-year: Courses & Grades (C&G)

### 1.1 Where coursework is asked (VERIFIED)
Source: Common App "Counselor conversations: Academic record reporting", Nov 2025 (2025-26 cycle) https://www.commonapp.org/files/webinar_decks/Academic-Record-Reporting.pdf
- In-progress coursework: Education > "Current or Most Recent Year Courses" (always asked).
- Completed coursework: "Courses & Grades" ONLY if a college on the student's list requires it ("captures courses with credit awarded on high school transcript").
- College attendance (dual enrollment etc.): Education > "Colleges & Universities" (up to 3 colleges; no course list asked).
- 2025-26: 77 colleges use C&G, 38 use STARS (the separate Self-reported Transcript and Academic Record System, formerly SRAR/SSAR).
- Current list of colleges requiring C&G (Oct 2026): https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/Do-all-members-of-The-Common-Application-use-Courses-Grades-in-their-admissions-process . It includes Stanford, Purdue, Amherst, CMU, Arizona State, U Arizona, U Oregon, UW-Madison, U Minnesota Twin Cities, U New Mexico, LMU, Morehouse. It does NOT include UT Austin.
- Requirements grid, 2026-27 first-year: https://content.commonapp.org/Files/ReqGrid.pdf (updated 09-25-2026; I did not parse it).

### 1.2 Fields (VERIFIED, same deck)
School level (required): School Name, School Year, Grading Scale, Schedule.
Per course row (required): Subject, Course Name, Course Level, at least one grade, at least one credit.

| Field | Type | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| School Name | dropdown | only schools already added in the Education section; extra transcripts via "Add Courses from Another Transcript" |
| School Year | dropdown | per grade grid; academic year (e.g., 2025-26) |
| Grade level | grid / tab | 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, plus "Other Courses" |
| Grading Scale | dropdown | list NOT VERIFIED; includes an "Other" option, after which grades are typed into the individual grade boxes (https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/What-if-my-Grading-Scale-doesn-t-appear-in-the-dropdown). Third-party counselor guides show "A-F" as one option (SECONDARY). |
| Schedule | dropdown | Semester, Trimester, Quarterly, Yearly (see 1.3) |

| Course Level | dropdown | see 1.4; if your level is missing, put it in the course title (https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/What-do-I-do-if-my-course-level-is-not-shown-in-the-choice-list) |
| Grade(s) | per-term boxes (typed or dropdown, NOT VERIFIED which) | one box per term of the chosen Schedule; blank allowed if transcript shows none; "do not add, average, or estimate" (https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/What-if-I-don-t-have-grades-for-every-term-for-a-course) |
| Credit(s) | per-term boxes, numeric (assumed) | unit not specified. "N/A" checkbox if the transcript lists no credit (https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/What-can-I-do-if-my-transcript-doesn-t-list-a-credit-for-a-particular-course-I-took) |

A counselor guide notes final grade and final credit boxes are left blank for courses at the home school when the term breakdown is entered (Lake Washington SD guide, 2022-23, SECONDARY: https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1666017842/lwsdorg/vqcysuatvtogz0wkjal3/CommonApplicationQuickGuide22-231.pdf). Treat as unverified.

### 1.3 Schedule / term values (VERIFIED, deck above)
Schedule options (called "Course scheduling system" in the Education section): **Semester, Trimester, Quarterly, Yearly**.
Terms shown are dynamic by schedule:
- Semester: S1, S2, Full Year
- Trimester: T1, T2, T3, Full Year
- Quarterly: Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Full Year
- Yearly: Full Year

Note: the school-details sheet (https://www.commonapp.org/static/551c899179247debfb36c77eecb36179/Resource_FY_HighSchoolDetails_ENG_2025.06.24_0.pdf, June 2025) labels the same choice "Course scheduling system: Semester, Trimester, Quarter" (no "Yearly"), so labels differ slightly by screen.

5. AS/A-level/International A-level, Cambridge AICE (printed wrapped across 2 lines; I read it as ONE option, but it could be two)
6. College Prep

10. Gifted

15. Pre-IB
16. Regents

(As read this is 17 labels; if items 5 and 9 are each two separate options it is 19. The exact split of items 5 and 9 is NOT VERIFIED.)
Dual enrollment rule: only college courses that appear on the HS transcript go in C&G; choose the HS that awarded the credit and Course Level = "Dual Enrollment" (https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/How-do-I-report-a-college-course-I-took-during-high-school-in-Courses-Grades). There is NO separate AP-vs-honors weighting field; weighting is only captured at the school level (GPA weighted/unweighted).

### 1.5 Subject values
**NOT VERIFIED.** Searches of Common App help, counselor decks and ~8 counselor/consultant guides found only "select the Subject" (no list). Do not assume a hierarchy (e.g., Fine Arts vs Performing Arts sub-types); I found none documented. Needs a live-account screenshot.

### 1.6 Grade values
NOT VERIFIED. Known: the scale dropdown has an "Other" escape hatch with free-typed grades. Pass/Fail handling: not documented.

### 1.7 Mapping-relevant rules
- **GPA is a separate question** (Education > Grades): "GPA scale" dropdown with options **4, 5, 6, ... 20, 100, None/N/A** (school details sheet above); GPA weighting = Weighted/Unweighted; if the school reports both, report weighted; a weighted GPA may exceed the scale number (e.g., report 5.0 on a "4" scale). Source: https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/How-do-I-report-my-class-rank-and-GPA (page dated 2026). Class rank reporting: Exact, Decile, Quintile, Quartile, None.
- No official statement found that Common App recalculates GPA from C&G entries (NOT VERIFIED either way).
- Years covered: grade grids for 9th, 10th, 11th (a grade with no courses is marked via the "I have reported all of my courses for this grade" box), and 12th: answer "Do you have 12th grade courses on your transcript with official grades?" Yes/No. 9th grade IS included. (https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/What-if-I-don-t-have-courses-to-list-for-a-particular-grade-in-the-Courses-and-Grades-section)
- Pre-9th, post-12th and summer courses: "Other Courses" section, only if on the HS transcript (https://appsupport.commonapp.org/applicantsupport/s/article/How-do-I-report-courses-taken-outside-of-my-secondary-school-s-courses).
- In-progress/planned senior courses: NOT in C&G until they have official grades; they are entered in Education > "Current or Most Recent Year Courses" (same Schedule/Course Level lists, per the deck). No explicit in-progress flag; an in-progress course is simply a course in that section without a grade.
- Transcript upload does NOT replace entry. Students must still send official transcripts; C&G is an additional self-report for colleges that require it (dual-enrollment article above).
- Entries must match the transcript exactly; no conversion, averaging or estimation.

## 2. University of California application: Academic History (first-year)

Official sources (cycle 2026-27, fall 2027 entry):
- Quick Reference Guide to UC Admissions, Aug 2026 (QRG): https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/counselors/_files/documents/quick-reference-guide-to-uc-admissions.pdf (pp. 10-12, 16-22, 58-62)
- "Presenting yourself on the first-year UC application 2026-27": https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/counselors/_files/documents/presenting-yourself-on-the-uc-application-first-year.pdf
- "Decoding coursework entries in the UC application", UC Counselor Conference, Sept 2026: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/counselors/_files/documents/2026-counselor-conference-ada-pdfs/cc_-decoding-coursework-entry_ada.pdf
- UC Davis application tips (honors codes): https://www.ucdavis.edu/admissions/undergraduate/first-year/application-tips
- SECONDARY walkthrough with field/option wording (undated, may be pre-2026): https://egelloc.com/new-uc-application-breakdown-academic-history/ and https://ivymax.com/uc-application-guide/

### 2.1 Structure (VERIFIED)
Academic History is organized as:
1. 7th/8th grade (math and LOTE only; only these two subject areas can be met with 7th-8th grade work; grade C or better; **students do not enter grades** for these; math = course name dropdown + term period; LOTE = course name typed + term period + course category (level) + language).
2. High school(s): Add each school (search by name/city/code; or manual entry; "Specialized curriculum": Home school, Independent study). Per school: start/end dates, grade levels with academic years, summer-course checkboxes (summer before 9th included), **grading system (select all that apply)** and **term system (select all that apply; based on the final grades received per year)**.
3. Courses by grade level 9, 10, 11, 12 (and 13 if applicable).
4. Colleges attended while in high school (separate tab; do not duplicate in the HS section even if posted on HS transcript).
5. "Additional comments" (550 chars). "Other coursework" (non-A-G, e.g., career/vocational, religious studies, language school; name, 350-char description, grade, time spent) lives in Activities & awards.

### 2.2 Per-course fields and values

| Item | Type | Detail | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grade level / academic year | tab + school-year mapping | 9-12 (+13 shifted back to 12th for 13-year systems) | VERIFIED (QRG p.61) |
| Subject area | dropdown (chosen first; a CA school's course list is then pre-populated) | A-G areas, see 2.3 | area names VERIFIED in QRG; exact dropdown strings NOT VERIFIED |
| Course name | CA schools: choose from the school's UC-approved A-G list. Others: free text, exactly as on transcript | | VERIFIED |
| Honors type | dropdown | **AP, HL (UC-approved honors level), IB, NH (Not honors)**; plus **CL** for UC-transferable college courses (UC Davis page); the 7th-8th LOTE entry has a separate "course category (level)" | AP/HL/IB/NH VERIFIED (UC Sept 2026); CL VERIFIED (UC Davis) |
| Term grades | one box per term of the chosen term system | grade codes below | VERIFIED |
| Credit | **no per-course credit field documented** | UC counts A-G courses by term (a "semester course" = one grade). Quarter courses /2 and trimester /1.5 for admissions-index math only. Summer is the last term of the academic year. | NOT VERIFIED that none exists; none described in 3 official docs |

### 2.3 Subject areas (A-G; QRG pp.11-12, Aug 2026, VERIFIED)
- (A) History/social science
- (B) English
- (C) Mathematics
- (D) Science (older UC text calls it "Laboratory science")
- (E) Language other than English (LOTE)
- (F) Visual and performing arts (VPA)
- (G) Additional college-preparatory elective

**Hierarchy: yes, at policy level.** QRG lists "disciplines" per area:
- C: Algebra I or Math I; Geometry or Math II; Algebra II or Math III; Precalculus; Calculus; Other Advanced Math.
- D: Biology/Life Sciences; Biology/Earth & Space Sciences; Chemistry; Chemistry/Earth & Space Sciences; Earth & Space Sciences; Integrated Science 1, 2, 3; Interdisciplinary Sciences; Physics; Physics/Earth & Space Sciences. Third/fourth-year-only: Applied Science, Computer Science, Engineering (and others).
- F: **Dance, Music, Theater, Visual Arts, Interdisciplinary Arts** (a student needs a yearlong course, or two semesters, from the SAME discipline). So "Fine Arts" vs "Performing Arts" does not exist in UC; it is one area (F) with 5 disciplines.
- Whether the application's manual-entry dropdown asks for the discipline: NOT VERIFIED.
- The SECONDARY egelloc walkthrough lists 9th-grade subject-area options as "History/Social Science, English, Science, Language Other Than English, Visual and Performing Arts, College-Prep Electives" (Math missing, likely a transcript omission).
- Not reportable in A-G: PE, teacher/office assistant, yearbook etc. unless on the A-G list.

### 2.4 Grade values (VERIFIED, UC Sept 2026 + Presenting-yourself 2026-27)
- Per term: letter grades A-F, numeric (0-100), "etc." by grading system. Enter exactly as on transcript.
- **UC ignores +/- for high school work**: B+, B, B- all enter as "B" (3 points). +/- IS used for college courses if the college awards them.
- **Pass/Credit/No Pass/No Credit "are not accepted"** for A-G (entered grade; cannot meet subject requirements).
- Special codes: **NO = Not planned / no course this term** (use for the term in which a one-term course was not taken), **IP = In Progress**, **PL = Planned** (12th grade defaults to IP/PL; do not enter senior grades unless already graduated).
- Grading system options (SECONDARY, ivymax, undated): "A B C D F (4.0 scale)", "0-100 (numeric scale)", "1-7 (IB scale)", Other. UC counselor deck says only "A-F, 0-100, etc.", and "Other may include narrative or international marks". Exact dropdown strings NOT VERIFIED.
- Term system options (SECONDARY, ivymax): Full year, Semester, Trimester, Quarter; colleges: Semester, Quarter, Trimester, Other. Official docs only say "term system based on the final grades received; multiple allowed (e.g., block scheduling)". Exact strings NOT VERIFIED.
- Repeats: report BOTH original and repeat course/grade (repeat only allowed to replace D/F; QRG p.21).

### 2.5 Rules that affect mapping (VERIFIED, QRG Aug 2026)
- **UC computes its own GPA** from A-G courses with letter grades in grades 10 and 11 (including summers after 9, 10, 11). Grade 9 is reported but NOT in the GPA. A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, F=0, no rounding.
- Honors points: +1 per semester for UC-approved honors (HL), designated AP and IB (all AP, "some" IB), and UC-transferable college courses with C or better (one college course = one grade and one honors point). Capped at 8 semesters, max 4 semesters in grade 10 ("weighted and capped GPA"). Campuses also see a fully weighted and an unweighted GPA. D/F in honors get no point. Nonresident: honors weight only for AP/IB.
- Students enter honors flags on each course; points are not added course by course.
- Years: every academic course from 9th grade onward, all schools attended; 7th-8th only math/LOTE. Summer courses must be reported (checkbox per grade).
- College/dual enrollment: reported only in the "Colleges attended in high school" tab as on the official college transcript. For UC credit: UC-transferable, at least 3 semester / 4 quarter units, letter grade, C or better (not C-). Non-A-G or under-3-unit courses go to Activities & awards "Other coursework". Entering a college: school, dates, term system, grading system; senior in-progress/planned allowed (IP/PL).
- 12th grade: in-progress/planned via IP/PL; the application cannot be edited after submit (Academic History is locked), but updates are reported separately.
- California Colleges Guidance Initiative (CCGI): students at CCGI partner schools may IMPORT courses and grades from CaliforniaColleges.edu (via 10-digit SSID in About you). Must verify all imports. This replaces manual entry, but NOT the later official transcript.
- Transcripts are not sent at application time; official records are required only after the student accepts an offer ("Unless requested ... not required prior to admission"). UC randomly verifies Academic History before admission.
- Non-California / international: no pre-approved list; type course names and grades exactly as on transcript, without converting international grades. QRG p.58.

## 3. ApplyTexas and UT Austin

### 3.1 ApplyTexas (official, accessed 2026-10-26 on the redesigned 2026-27 site)
Sources: https://www.applytexas.org/application-guide/ and https://www.applytexas.org/faqs/
- Application flow = screener questions -> **Core Questions** (shared across all schools) -> school-specific questions/essays. The guide does not list Core Question fields; the "Preview application questions" accordion exposes no content without an account. **NOT VERIFIED**: whether Core Questions contain any course-level entry. No official source I found documents course fields, dropdowns, course levels, credit units or terms for ApplyTexas.
- Official guide statements (VERIFIED): "You cannot upload transcripts through the ApplyTexas portal." Colleges ask for official transcripts separately. Some schools "may also ask for a Self-Reported Academic Record (SRAR). The school collects these documents."
- FAQ (VERIFIED): for Texas public high school grads, the transcript can be requested when completing the application; private-school, out-of-state, or college-credit students send transcripts separately.
- State direct admissions (My Texas Future, TEA Aug 2026 notice https://tea.texas.gov/educators/superintendents/graduation-requirements-and-my-texas-future-portal-published-august-20-2026.pdf): students self-report in My Texas Future a **graduation plan type** and at least one of **class rank, GPA, SAT total, ACT composite**. No course-level data. It connects to ApplyTexas "reducing the information students need to enter manually". Graduation requirement (SB 2314) applies to students enrolled in 2026-27.
- Old ApplyTexas topic forms (Topic A/B, pre-2026) are not covered by an official page I could reach. A third-party guide (instateutaustin.com, not affiliated, no sourcing) claims courses are listed by year; treat as NOT VERIFIED.

### 3.2 UT Austin (official, https://admissions.utexas.edu/apply/freshman/, accessed 2026-10-26)
- Summer/fall first-year application: **Common App** (ApplyTexas is also listed as an option). UT is NOT on the Common App C&G-required list, so students do not enter courses and grades in Common App C&G for UT.
- Academic record = official high school transcript with grades 9-11 coursework and class rank (position out of class total, e.g., 5/130). UT checks prerequisites against the transcript (https://admissions.utexas.edu/apply/application-materials/high-school-prerequisites/ : 4 English, 3 math (4 rec.), 2 science (4 rec.), 3 social studies (4 rec.), 2 foreign language, 1 PE (rec.), 1 fine arts (rec.), 6 electives (rec.); credits in units, dual credit counts).
- **"Academic Coursework Entry Form"**: "Students who do not attend a Texas high school may be required to input their high school courses via the Academic Coursework Entry Form. If applicable, MyStatus will prompt you." Fields, dropdowns, terms and credit units are **NOT VERIFIED** (login-gated; also not described on any public page). Email admissions@austin.utexas.edu / 512-475-7399 for the field spec.
- Dual credit: must be reported as a transfer institution; official college transcript required (https://admissions.utexas.edu/apply/application-materials/transcript-info/). Test scores self-reported in MyStatus after official request (not coursework).
- GPA: not asked on the UT page; no information on UT-recalculated GPA found (UT uses class rank and its own holistic review: NOT VERIFIED beyond that).
