# Curricula & exams research (2026-11-05)

Tags: [official] fetched from the issuer's page; [summary] from a search snippet only; [secondary] third-party; [knowledge] from model knowledge, NOT verified this session.

## Summary (read this first)
1. One record shape fits everything: curriculum + subject + level + session (year+month) + status (planned / predicted / final / self-reported) + grade string exactly as printed + scale id. Keep grade as text.
2. Scales: AP 1-5; IB subject 1-7, core TOK/EE A-E, total 0-45 (24 minimum); A-level A*-E, AS A-E (Cambridge AS lowercase a-e), GCSE England 9-1, IGCSE A*-G or 9-1; GSAT 0-15 per subject (級分); HKDSE 5** to 1 [knowledge].
3. Predicted grades: UCAS yes (teacher/referee gives them; applicant never types them). IB yes (teacher, confirmed by coordinator). AP: no predicted-score concept found; UC asks for planned AP exams (date only) and accepts IB predicted scores.
4. UCAS Education section: one entry per subject; date = award date; unfinished = "pending"; Cambridge awarding organisation "CIE".
5. Common App Testing: self-reported; families AP Subject Tests, IB Subject Tests, Cambridge Tests (plus SAT/ACT, English tests). Exact field names and entry cap NOT found.
6. Not found: Common App Testing fields/cap; UC AP/IB/international-exam field names; official IB subject list (ibo.org returns 403); IB predicted-grade deadlines (sources conflict); Canada/Australia forms.

## 2. IB Diploma Programme
- Scale and total [summary of the ibo.org DP passing criteria page, https://www.ibo.org/about-the-ib/what-it-means-to-be-an-ib-student/recognizing-student-achievement/about-assessment/dp-passing-criteria/ ; the page itself returned 403]: six subjects graded 1-7 (max 42) + core up to 3 bonus points = 45 max; diploma needs >=24 points; an E in TOK or EE is a failing condition. TOK and EE are each A-E and combined on a matrix. Matrix values conflict between secondary sources; the one official example: TOK B + EE C = 2 points. Take the matrix only from the official IB document (not obtained). Other failing conditions (CAS, grade 1 in a subject) not verified [knowledge].
- EE marked out of 34 on five criteria [summary]. EE guide (first assessment 2027): https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/extended-essay-first-assessment-2027-guide-sbs.pdf (not read).
- CAS pass/fail: [knowledge], not verified. Sessions May and November [knowledge; summary of an IB page mentioning both].
- Predicted grades: set by subject teachers, confirmed by the IB coordinator [secondary]. Deadlines conflict: 20 Apr / 20 Oct on an IB on-screen-exam page [summary]; 10 Apr / 10 Oct in a teacher resource; mid-March / mid-September on a Pamoja help page. Treat as school-specific.
- IB Career-related Programme and courses-only candidates (certificates): NOT researched.
- Subject list [secondary: https://cognito.org/blog/full-list-of-ib-subjects, fetched 2026-11-05; ibo.org unreachable]. Verify against ibo.org before shipping:
  - Group 1 Studies in language and literature: Language A: Literature (SL, HL); Language A: Language and Literature (SL, HL); Literature and Performance (SL only)
  - Group 2 Language acquisition: Language B (SL, HL); Language ab initio (SL only); Latin (SL, HL); Classical Greek (SL, HL)
  - Group 3 Individuals and societies: Business Management; Digital Society (first assessment 2024); Economics; Environmental Systems and Societies (also counts as Group 4; HL from first assessment 2026); Geography; Global Politics; History; Philosophy; Psychology; Social and Cultural Anthropology (all SL, HL); World Religions (SL only)
  - Group 4 Sciences: Biology; Chemistry; Computer Science; Design Technology; Environmental Systems and Societies; Physics; Sports, Exercise and Health Science (all SL, HL)
  - Group 5 Mathematics: Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (SL, HL); Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation (SL, HL)
  - Group 6 The arts (optional): Dance; Film; Music; Theatre; Visual Arts (SL, HL)
  - That is 34 distinct course names (ESS counted once). The 40-subject target is met only if Language A / Language B are expanded by language (each language is its own subject, e.g. "Chinese A: Literature"; that language list was NOT obtained). Full official list URL: not found.

## 3. A-levels and relatives
- A-level A*-E, AS A-E (England) [summary: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gcse-9-to-1-grade-scale-explained/gcse-9-to-1-grade-scale-explained and Ofqual material].
- Cambridge International [summary of cambridgeinternational.org pages]: A Level A*-E; AS Level a-e (lowercase, no A*); series June and November (March in India); E / e / G are minimum passing grades; "Ungraded" below that; staged route: an A-level candidate missing E may receive an AS grade.
- Exam boards: AQA, OCR, Pearson Edexcel, WJEC/Eduqas, Cambridge International [knowledge]; not verified.
- UCAS predicted grades: entered by referee/centre, applicant does not see them; cannot be changed after sending; the amendment form accepts received results only [summary: https://www.ucas.com/advisers/help-and-training/guides-resources-and-training/application-overview/our-adviser-portal/managing-references-and-predicted-grades-adviser-portal , https://www.ucas.com/forms/request-amend-qualifications-listed-your-application].
- UCAS tariff (new structure, 2026 cycle) [summary: https://www.ucas.com/applying/before-you-apply/what-and-where-to-study/entry-requirements/calculate-your-ucas-tariff-points]: A level A* = 56 points; Scottish Higher A 33 / B 27 / C 21 / D 15; the IB Diploma itself earns no tariff points; EPQ points not found. Many universities do not use tariff points.
- EPQ grades A*-E [knowledge], not verified.

## 4. GCSE / IGCSE / O-level
- GCSE England 9-1 since 2017, replacing A*-G; Wales/Northern Ireland keep A*-G [summary: gov.uk / Ofqual].
- Cambridge IGCSE: graded A*-G; some syllabuses/zones also 9-1; tiered papers can use restricted sets (A*-E or 9-3) [summary: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/412121-igcse-9-1-grading-factsheet.pdf]. Series June, November, March.
- UCAS entry for Cambridge quals [official PDF, read 2026-11-05: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/563572-guidance-for-learners-applying-through-ucas-with-cambridge-international-qualifications.pdf]: Step 1 choose type "International and EU"; Step 2 AS/A Level, IPQ, AICE Diploma -> "International A Level"; IGCSE and O Level -> "International GCSE"; Step 3 subject typed or dropdown ("other" if missing), date (achieved or future), awarding organisation "CIE", grade or "pending".

## 6. Application forms
- Common App Testing [official PDF, read 2026-11-05: https://www.commonapp.org/static/d5fca99aebaebd5bee20bb26d2edc802/Resource_FY_Testing_ENG_2025.06.25_0.pdf]: the section self-reports standardized tests. Under "Subject tests": SAT Subject Tests, AP Subject Tests, IB Subject Tests, Cambridge Tests. English tests: Cambridge English, Duolingo English Test, IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL. Scores are self-reported; official scores go via the testing agency. Tests can be removed under "Tests Taken". NOT FOUND: field names for AP/IB/Cambridge, max entries, whether future tests are allowed. A 2014 blog (secondary, unreliable) said the form asks a count, then per test a date (MMYY), subject, score. There is no separate "A-level" or "other national exam" type in the official list [inference]. Courses & Grades interaction not researched.
- UC application [summary: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/how-to-apply/applying-as-a-first-year/filling-out-the-application.html and UC freshman guide]: Test scores section has separate pages for ACT/SAT, AP, IB, English proficiency, International exams. Report completed exams and "indicate if you're planning on taking an exam in the future". For repeats, enter the highest composite from one sitting. IB predicted scores, if the school releases them, go on the IB Exams page; none at submission means no need to report later. Official AP/IB scores required after accepting an offer (UC Merced page: July 15 fall / Dec 31 spring, secondary). Field names not found.
- UCAS Education section [official Cambridge guidance + summaries of UCAS pages]: one entry per subject; each: qualification type, subject, awarding organisation, date (award date, not exam date), grade or "pending". International schools: deselect the school's country, then pick the qualification [summary]. IB Diploma: search "IB", enter overall score if complete, then each subject separately [summary]. Whether non-UK applicants must list GCSEs: not found.

## 7. Suggested storage pattern
- exam_record: id, user_id, curriculum (ap | ib | alevel_england | alevel_cie | alevel_edexcel_intl | gcse | igcse | olevel | gsat | other), level (AP exam, IB SL, IB HL, A level, AS level, IGCSE, ...), subject (official name), session_year, session_month, status (planned | predicted | final | self_reported), grade_text (as printed), scale_id (ap_1_5, ib_1_7, ib_core_ae, alevel_astar_e, as_a_e_lower, gcse_9_1, igcse_astar_g, gsat_0_15 ...), awarding_body, notes.
- IB extras: diploma total (0-45), TOK grade, EE grade, CAS flag. AP extras: Capstone, WE Service, Scholar are awards, not scores.
- Predicted + final for the same subject: either two rows or one row with predicted_grade and final_grade; the single row maps to UCAS "pending" -> achieved.
- Awarding body: UCAS guidance says "CIE"; a secondary note says "CAIE" for 2017+; store a display name and map at export.
