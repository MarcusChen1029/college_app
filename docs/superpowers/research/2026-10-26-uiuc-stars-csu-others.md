## 0. Headline answer: what is "STARS"?

**STARS = "Self-reported Transcript and Academic Record System"**, the new name (effective 2025-08-01) of the system formerly called **SRAR** (Self-Reported Academic Record) and **SSAR** (Self-reported Student Academic Record). It is a single third-party website where the student enters coursework once and links the record to many colleges. Evidence:

- Official STARS help center, article "SRAR/SSAR is STARS: What You Need to Know": renamed to STARS, official transition 2025-08-01 (article last updated 2025-07-31), launched together with an AI-OCR "STARS EasyImport" tool; accounts/records unchanged. Footer "Powered by ScarletCS" (inferred operator). https://starsrecord.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/35076353058971-SRAR-SSAR-is-STARS-What-You-Need-to-Know
- Common App's Nov 2025 counselor deck: "STARS (the Self-reported Transcript and Academic Record System, formerly known as SRAR or SSAR)"; in 2025-26, 77 colleges use Common App "Courses & Grades" and 38 colleges use STARS. https://www.commonapp.org/files/webinar_decks/Academic-Record-Reporting.pdf
- Official "Which Colleges Use STARS" (page dated 2026-08-06): 39 institutions, including Texas A&M, Penn State, NYU, Rutgers (3 campuses), Temple, U Florida, FSU, USF, UW Seattle, Virginia Tech, UConn, UMass Amherst, U Minnesota Twin Cities, U Oregon, U Pittsburgh, U Delaware, Clemson, Baylor, LSU, UT Arlington, UT San Antonio, SUNY Buffalo, USAF Academy. **UIUC, Georgia Tech, Purdue are NOT listed.** https://starsrecord.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/35363280510235-Which-Colleges-Use-STARS
- Help center root: https://starsrecord.zendesk.com/hc/en-us (categories: Using STARS, First-Year, International, Transfer, Submission & Linking, High School Counselors). Login site (per secondary source, URL unconfirmed on an official page except as named in the rename article): srar.selfreportedtranscript.com

**Why the user's list "Common App, UC, UT, UIUC, stars" probably includes it:** very likely STARS exactly as above (a competitor/peer to Common App "Courses & Grades"). UT may be ApplyTexas (UT Austin) which is separate, and UIUC has its OWN in-house SRAR (not STARS). Candidates ranked by evidence:
1. STARS (formerly SRAR/SSAR) - strong, verified, and it is literally spelled STARS in 2025-26 materials.
2. "Stars" as a typo of "SRAR" (UIUC's own record is still named SRAR) - plausible because the user lists UIUC beside it.
3. Other things named STARS in college contexts (AASHE STARS sustainability rating, etc.) are unrelated to coursework. I did not find any other admissions system named STARS.
Do not assert which one the user meant; but STARS-the-system is the one worth building to.

## 1. UIUC (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign) - its own SRAR in myIllini

**How it collects coursework (VERIFIED):** First-years apply through Common App, then UIUC emails a link to its own **Self-Reported Academic Record (SRAR)** (inside the myIllini account) where courses and grades are entered. UIUC says it cannot use courses/grades entered in Common App's Courses & Grades. The 2027-cycle counselor guide says this stays the same ("SRAR will still be submitted separately through myIllini"). It is NOT STARS.
- https://www.admissions.illinois.edu/first-year-apply-faq/ (main SRAR rules)
- https://www.admissions.illinois.edu/first-year-apply/
- https://www.admissions.illinois.edu/hs-counselor-guide/
- Requirements page lists SRAR as required material: https://admissions.illinois.edu/apply/freshman/requirements
- Only video tutorial exists for the form UI (YouTube playlist linked from first-year-apply); not readable here.

**Rules stated on UIUC pages (VERIFIED, FAQ above):**
- Enter exactly as on transcript; "All semester, trimester, or quarter grades must be listed"; do not average. Block-scheduled schools: report courses as year-long and answer "Yes" to the block-scheduling question on the Education page.
- Years: 9th grade only if taken at a secondary school (not junior/middle); 7th/8th: only advanced math or foreign language. Multiple high schools listed separately (one record each).
- College/dual-credit courses may be self-reported; 1 college semester = 1 high-school year.
- Senior year: in-progress courses entered; changes after submission via "Course Change Form" in myIllini. Final transcript must match, else offer rescinded.
- Grading-scale selector (VERIFIED wording): option "A-F grades or numerical grades with a grading scale listed on the transcript" (then enter the conversion), or "Other" (enter your scale even if not on transcript). For several scales: pick "Other" under "Grading Scale/Conversion", then "New Scale/Conversion" for each extra.
- AP/IB/AS scores are not used in holistic review; the FAQ doesn't explain how AP/IB courses are flagged.

**NOT VERIFIED / NOT FOUND (UIUC official pages do not publish):** per-course dropdown names, course level/type list (honors/AP/IB/dual), subject-area list, credit units, grade value list, term-type dropdown, weighted vs unweighted rule. Only hint: Singapore page says IB students "select the level (IB SL or IB HL)", implying a level dropdown that includes IB SL/HL, but the complete list is not published. To get exact values: open a real myIllini SRAR (needs an applicant account) or ask admissions@illinois.edu, 217-333-0302.
- Subject pattern used for ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS (not necessarily the SRAR dropdown): English 4; Math 3 or 3.5 (4 recommended); Social Science 2 (4 rec.); Lab Science 2 (4 rec.); Language Other Than English 2 (4 rec.); Flexible Academic Units 2 (4+ rec.). Source: https://admissions.illinois.edu/apply/freshman/requirements

**International at UIUC (VERIFIED):**
- Same SRAR. For external-exam systems (O-Levels, IGCSE/GCE, Indian Std X, HKDSE, Singapore O/A) the applicant answers "Yes" to the externally-administered-exams question in Education, ticks in Courses "I only have international exam results during this period (or did not attend this school during years 9-11/grade 12)", and enters exams under **Other Academic Information > "International External Exam Results"** (exam type, e.g. Singapore A-Levels H1/H2; "Results Not Received Yet" for pending). If <=4 GCSE/O-Level exams and <=2 A-Level exams, report school-transcript courses instead. AS-Levels alone do not meet the standard. Source: https://www.admissions.illinois.edu/self-reported/gcse-gce and https://www.admissions.illinois.edu/singapore-application-help/ and FAQ above.
- Grading scale for exam systems: choose "Other" and type e.g. "External Exam-Singapore O/A-Level grading information".
- Other country pages exist: /self-reported/new-zealand, /self-reported/malaysia-courses (404 when fetched, may have moved), /self-reported/singapore-courses (404; replaced by singapore-application-help).
- Years: grades 9-12 (admitted students must send official records for every year reported).

## 2. STARS (ScarletCS) - detailed model

Key official help articles (all https://starsrecord.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/...):
- How To Manually Complete Your STARS Record: 37149481466907
- How To Manually Enter Your Coursework By Year: 36987878462107
- How Do I Select A Subject Area: 37027194180251
- How Do I Select A Generic Course Title: 37035522611611
- How Do I Select A Course Level: 37036006202523
- How To Select A Course Length: 37036235354779
- Which Grade Scale Should I Choose (US): 36921882761883
- Dual enrollment / college courses: 37195279297435
- International: grade scale 37316912737691; record-grade for intl school 37606351545499; education system 37495583395099; grade-level equivalents 37670532180251; what's different 39296157612059

**Record structure (VERIFIED):**
- School-level: high school(s), start month/year of 9th grade, last month/year, graduation month/year; **Grade Scale** (US: Letter Grades / Number Grades / Letter & Number Grades / Decimal Grades); cumulative GPA + class rank per school (as printed; numeric/percentile etc.); optional annual academic GPA per year (US schools only; checkbox "I have not received an annual GPA for this academic year").
- Number-to-letter conversion table may be requested on the Enter Coursework screen for numeric scales.
- Coverage: all courses grades 9-11, grade 12 (use "IP - In Progress" if no final grade, even if not started), withdrawn and repeated courses, dual-enrollment/college courses that appear on the HS transcript. Two entry modes: by year, or by subject area; plus AI "EasyImport" (OCR of uploaded transcript).

**Per-course fields, in order (VERIFIED, "Coursework By Year" article):** Subject Area -> Generic Course Title -> Course Name (as on transcript) -> Course Level -> Course Length -> Grade(s) -> Save. Credit value is entered too (see Course Length article). Weighted: "Use the unweighted grade if both weighted and unweighted are listed." Never average/convert.

**Subject Area (VERIFIED, exact, no sub-categories documented; a Generic Course Title list sits underneath each - titles themselves NOT retrieved):**
| # | Subject Area |
|---|---|
| 1 | English |
| 2 | Mathematics |
| 3 | Science |
| 4 | History / Social Studies |
| 5 | World Language |
| 6 | Business |
| 7 | Computer Science |
| 8 | Fine Arts / Music |
| 9 | Physical Education / Health / Gym |
| 10 | Other Subject Area |
Rule: Psychology and Economics go under Social Science/History even if school lists them under Science/Business. Generic Course Title offers "Other" for free text. (Source article 37027194180251.) Hierarchy = Subject Area (10) > Generic Course Title (many, not captured).

**Course Level (VERIFIED, partial - article says the dropdown "contains the full list"; named levels):** Honors; Advanced Placement (AP); International Baccalaureate (IB); Dual Enrollment; College Prep; Regents; Gifted and Talented; Standard (default when none apply). Also: PLTW has its own guidance (article 37070944232219). Dual Enrollment requires a college name and has alternates named in the article: Dual Credit (SDC), College in HS, College Credit Plus, Early College Experience (ECE). Rule: AP, IB, Dual Enrollment only if the transcript shows that exact designation. Full dropdown list: NOT VERIFIED.

**Course Length (VERIFIED exact):** Full Year; Semester; Trimester; Quarterly. Match to how the school issues FINAL GRADES, not class duration. If a transcript shows term grades plus a final year grade, choose Full Year and record only the final. One-term course: fill that term only. International adds "External Exam" (and one article mentions a "quintile" option - NOT fully verified).

**Credits (VERIFIED):** entered per course as printed on transcript; in-progress courses: credit expected; never 0 for an ongoing course; if transcript has no credits keep STARS default. Credit unit type (e.g., Carnegie units vs hours): NOT VERIFIED.

**Grade values (partial):** Letter scale examples A+, A, A-, B+ ... (full list NOT VERIFIED); Number 0-100 whole numbers; Decimal e.g. 89.5; Letter & Number combined; "IP - In Progress"; Pass/Fail used for middle-school math/language (Penn State page). 

**Term types:** Full Year, Semester, Trimester, Quarterly (above). Academic years Grade 9-12 (+ "Equivalent to U.S. 9th Grade" for middle school credit; summer courses and high-school-credit-in-middle-school have their own articles 37023697628827, 37022645100699).

**International in STARS (VERIFIED):**
- Required for international first-years at several schools: UW "All first-year applicants - including U.S., international and homeschool" must self-report via STARS (https://admit.washington.edu/apply/freshman/how-to-apply/common-app/); Penn State: all international first-years except South Korean system (official transcripts) (https://www.psu.edu/resources/international-students/self-reported-transcript-academic-record-system). URI: only if American-style curriculum (https://web.uri.edu/admission/?p=34384, per search summary). FAU: US or US-modelled curriculum.
- Grade scale for international (official, https://starsrecord.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/37316912737691): Letter Grades (incl. A*, A1, A2); Number Grades (0-100); Decimal Grades (89.5); Letters and Numbers; Narrative/Descriptive (Excellent/Satisfactory/Poor; also numeric with odd maximums, e.g. max 600). Escape hatch: "Something Not Listed Here" -> enter grade + maximum possible grade (e.g. 110 of 150). Grades above 200 -> use the narrative/descriptive scale. A different scale can be set per course.
- Education system/curriculum picker (by region; examples IB, IGCSE, CBSE, WASSCE; each region has "Other"), set per school, per academic year, and per course. Full list NOT retrieved.
- Year mapping: 4-year secondary: yr1-4 = US 9-12; 3-year: yr1-3 = US 10-12; 9th grade in middle school = "Equivalent to U.S. 9th Grade". Extra fields: Language of instruction; predicted scores for in-progress; external/leaving exams (A-Levels, IB, CBSE) via "Enter Exams" / "International Leaving Exams"; proficiency levels such as GCE or IGCSE; no annual GPA for international schools.
- 7-point IB scale and A-level specifics: NOT VERIFIED (not addressed in articles retrieved; handled via education-system + proficiency level + grade scale choice).

## 3. Cal State Apply (CSU) - Liaison

Official Applicant Help Center, section Academic History: https://help.liaisonedu.com/Cal_State_Apply_Applicant_Help_Center/Filling_Out_Your_Cal_State_Apply_Application/Cal_State_Apply_Academic_History (page versions show 2027 and 2028 application years; section for 2028 opens 2026-10-01).

**High School Coursework entry (VERIFIED, article 03_High_School_Coursework):**
- Fields per course: course title (type-ahead for California schools; manual exactly as on transcript for non-California), Grade Level (dropdown "Add Grade Level"; 7th/8th allowed when they satisfied A-G, paired with 9th; summer courses entered under next grade level, e.g. summer between 9 and 10 = 10th), Year (auto from High Schools Attended), per-term grade, course type, summer slider ("Is this a Summer Course") / "Summer 1/Summer 2" labels, A-G area (right column on "Update A-G Courses").
- **Course Type dropdown (VERIFIED, exact):** None (standard); Advanced Placement (+1 GPA point); International Baccalaureate (+1); Honors (approved honors, +1). Honors points apply to approved courses only.
- **Grade per term (VERIFIED):** letter grade; Pass; In Progress; Planned; No Grade / No Course.
- Repeats: report only the highest grade; if repeat is in progress/planned, original grade counts until new grade posts.
- In-progress/planned: optional but strongly recommended; any term containing In Progress/Planned cannot be marked complete (whole term marked In Progress/Planned).
- Credits: high school credit field exists ("courses, grades and credits earned") but unit definition NOT VERIFIED.
- Concurrent college courses go in College Coursework section, not here.
- Applicants who attended ONLY foreign high schools skip High School Coursework Entry and A-G Matching (they provide different academic info).

**A-G subject areas (partial, VERIFIED page 07_A-G_Matching):** Area C Mathematics; D1 Biological Science; D2 Physical Science; E Language Other Than English (LOTE); G College-Preparatory Elective (1 Year). Names for A, B, F are NOT given on that page (CSU/UC standard names are History/Social Science, English, Visual & Performing Arts but that is from memory - NOT VERIFIED here). Courses without an A-G area do not count in GPA. Source: https://help.liaisonedu.com/Cal_State_Apply_Applicant_Help_Center/Filling_Out_Your_Cal_State_Apply_Application/Cal_State_Apply_Academic_History/07_A-G_Matching

**Grade values (VERIFIED, 10_Cal_State_Apply_Grade_Values_Chart):** transcript A+, A -> A (4.0); A- 3.7; AB 3.5; B+ 3.3; B 3.0; B- 2.7; BC 2.5; C+ 2.3; C 2.0; C- 1.7; CD 1.5; D+ 1.3; D 1.0; D- 0.7; E, F, WF -> F (0). US number grades 4.0..0.0 map to those; US percentage: 90-100 A, 80-89 B, 70-79 C, 60-69 D, <=59 F. Canadian percentage: 90-100 A+, 80-89 A, 70-79 B, 60-69 C, 50-59 D, <=49 F. Rounding up to the higher grade when between values. CAS Grade auto-populates and is not editable. Page covers no other national systems.

**International:** Q on Academic Information page: "attended high school/secondary school outside the United States?" yes -> enter academic performance and diploma info instead of course list. No international grade scale conversion documented. Transcript entry (college coursework, 04/05 articles): enter exactly as on transcript, Pass = "P", Credit = "CR"; credits as semester/quarter hours; Canadian credit-unit conversions listed. Term types for college coursework not verified.

## 4. Common App "Courses & Grades" (baseline, since UIUC/STARS exist beside it)

Source: Common App Academic Record Reporting deck, Nov 2025 (VERIFIED): https://www.commonapp.org/files/webinar_decks/Academic-Record-Reporting.pdf
- Required once per school: School Name, School Year, Grading Scale, Schedule. Required per row: Subject, Course Name, Course Level, at least one grade, at least one credit.
- **Schedule (course scheduling system):** Semester (S1, S2, Full Year); Trimester (T1, T2, T3, Full Year); Quarterly (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Full Year); Yearly (Full Year).
- **Course Level (full list in the deck):** Regular/Standard; Accelerated; Advanced; Advanced Placement (AP); AS/A-level/International A-level, Cambridge AICE; College Prep; Dual Enrollment; Enriched; GCSE, IGCSE; Gifted; High Honors; Honors; Intensive; International Baccalaureate (IB); Pre-IB; Regents; N/A.
- Grade-level grids (9th-12th, plus "Other Courses" for middle school/post-12th/summer on the HS transcript). 12th grade current-year courses are in Education > "Current or Most Recent Year Courses". Only courses with HS credit.
- Common App GPA scale dropdown: 4 through 20, plus 100, else "N/A" (https://www.commonapp.org/static/551c899179247debfb36c77eecb36179/Resource_FY_HighSchoolDetails_ENG_2025.06.24_0.pdf, per search summary).
- Subject dropdown values, grading-scale dropdown values, credit units: NOT VERIFIED (Common App support pages are JS-rendered and returned empty).
- 77 colleges use it for 2025-26 (deck). Georgia Tech: Common App only; no general course self-report page found (it self-reports dual enrollment and test scores). Purdue: reads "courses and grades self-reported on Common App or on a transcript" (admissions.purdue.edu first-year criteria; search summary).

## 6. International applicants and grade scales (summary)
- STARS: best-documented. Letter / 0-100 number / decimal / letter+number / narrative; "Something Not Listed Here" with max possible grade; per-course scale; education-system picker; external exams (IB, A-levels, IGCSE, CBSE) with predicted grades. Required for international at UW and Penn State.
- UIUC SRAR: free "Other" grading scale text; exam-based systems go in "International External Exam Results"; transcript courses otherwise as printed.
- CSU: foreign HS applicants skip course entry; only US and Canadian conversion tables.
- Common App: GPA scale 4-20 or 100 or N/A; course levels include GCSE/IGCSE, AS/A-level, Cambridge AICE, IB, Pre-IB.
- Nothing found that publishes how a 7-point IB or 5.0 scale is mapped inside STARS or UIUC SRAR (treat as school-specific free entry).

## 7. Ranked support list (for the data model: period, grade, grade scale, level, credits, category/sub-category)
1. **Common App Courses & Grades** - largest base (77 colleges 2025-26), fully enumerated Schedule and Course Level lists; model's period/level/credit fields map 1:1.
2. **STARS** - 39 colleges incl. UW, TAMU, Penn State, NYU, Rutgers; one record reused across schools; two-level category (Subject Area > Generic Course Title), 4 course lengths, international grade-scale types, so the richest superset.
3. **UIUC SRAR** - high-demand school, own form; need to map grade-scale "Other" and year-long/block rules; level/subject dropdowns still unknown (get from a live account).
4. **Cal State Apply (CSU)** - large public system; clear enums (4 course types, 5 grade statuses, A-G areas) but A-G subject structure and no international scale.
5. **UC Academic History** - A-G only, honors-point rules; dropdowns unverified.
6. **ApplyTexas / Scoir / Coalition** - lowest priority: no verified per-course enumerations found.
