# UK international applicants and everything after an offer (entry 2027)

Researched 2026-11-04 for the tracker's UK feature brainstorm. Scope: qualification recognition (US diploma + AP, IB, Taiwan diploma + GSAT), English, the post-offer path (deposit, CAS, visa), money, and policy changes. The UCAS form, personal statement, references, deadlines, tests, interviews and Firm/Insurance are covered by another agent.

## How to read this file

- Every claim has a source URL; all pages were fetched on 2026-11-04. A page's own date is given when it showed one ("page date"). "2026 entry" means the page describes the previous cycle, so 2027 values may differ.
- Source-quality tags: **[gov]** gov.uk / UKVI, **[uni]** the university's own page, **[summary]** a search-tool summary of an official page whose full text I did not read (treat as unverified until re-checked), **[secondary]** UKCISA / UUK / press, **[agent]** commercial counsellor or blog.
- "Not found" means I did not obtain it. Nothing here is filled from memory. Tool problems: Oxford (ox.ac.uk), UCL and Cardiff pages returned 403 to direct fetches, the LSE page is script-rendered, and the Firecrawl search credits ran out partway through. So Oxford, UCL, LSE, King's, Warwick, Bristol and Manchester are thin or missing (see section 1).

### 1.3 What this means

- The pattern in the five universities where I read the actual text (Cambridge, Imperial, Edinburgh, St Andrews, Birmingham) is: **a Taiwan national high school diploma, on its own, is usually not direct entry for the most selective universities.** Students are pointed to IB, A-level, AP (often five 5s at the top end) or a foundation year. Mid-ranked universities (Birmingham, Cardiff, LJMU) accept the diploma at about 75-80% average, and Birmingham alone, among those retrieved, accepts GSAT top-level scores for direct Year 1 entry.
- For **US-curriculum** students the common ask is AP scores (Imperial: 5,5,5,4,4; St Andrews: three at 4+ with 5s in required subjects), with SAT/ACT as a supplement at some (Imperial 1460 / 31 in the 3-AP route).
- **No retrieved page defines GSAT/AST conversion** beyond Birmingham's top-level rule. AST (分科測驗) is not mentioned on any retrieved page. Treat "GSAT accepted" as university-specific and rare.

### 1.4 Foundation years

- **Edinburgh IFP**: full-time, one year, for applicants not eligible for direct entry. Eligibility: not a native English speaker, school qualifications within the last 4 years, English test usually IELTS 5.5 overall with no band below 5.5 [summary]. Tuition: the fees page excerpt shows **GBP 31,100** (year not visible in my excerpt); a search summary quoted **GBP 29,600 for 2026/27**. Also budget at least GBP 300 for books and printing; full payment is due before the programme starts in September. Sources: https://cahss.ed.ac.uk/international-and-study-abroad/foundation-programme/applying-for-ifp/fees-and-finance and https://study.ed.ac.uk/undergraduate/entry-requirements/international-qualifications/international-foundation-programme
- **UCL UPC**: exists; fee and Taiwan-specific entry requirements not retrieved.
- Foundation adds **one extra year of cost** (Edinburgh IFP alone is about GBP 30k), and it is a separate application, so a "can I apply directly?" answer of "no, foundation" changes the whole budget.

## 2. English requirements and waivers

**For the visa [gov]** (https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/knowledge-of-english, no page date shown):
- Degree-level study needs CEFR **B2**. A Secure English Language Test (SELT) is **not automatically required**: a degree-level university can assess English itself, to a standard equivalent to B2.
- Other proofs: UK school qualification started before age 18; degree taught in English (with an Ecctis assessment code); approved test. Exempt: nationals of listed countries including the USA, Australia, Ireland, UK; Canadians; and those who already proved English in an earlier visa application.
- **Taiwan is not in the exempt list as quoted**, so a Taiwanese citizen at an American school in Taiwan does not get a visa waiver by nationality.

**For the university [uni]**:
- **Edinburgh** (https://www.ed.ac.uk/studying/undergraduate/entry-requirements/english-language): accepts IELTS Academic, IELTS Academic for UKVI and IELTS Academic Online (not IELTS General, IELTS for UKVI General or One Skill Retake); also Oxford ELLT, Oxford Test of English Advanced, TOEFL iBT and Trinity ISE. Requirements must be met **in a single test**. Test results "no more than two years old on the first of the month in which the degree begins". IB English (Standard Level at 5; English ab initio not accepted) and IB MYP English at 5 are accepted proofs. The page says accepted qualifications meet the visa standard. Whether PTE, Cambridge or Duolingo appear later on the page: **not read**.
- **Oxford** (graduate English page, as summarised): the two IELTS Academic versions differ only in format; either accepted; Oxford recommends standard Academic because the UKVI version costs more and is less available; tests must be less than two years old at course start [summary]. UG-specific page was 403.
- **Newcastle** [summary]: typical UG is IELTS 6.5 overall with 5.5 in each skill; for visa applicants it accepts only listed UKVI-approved tests. **Queen Mary** [summary]: for bachelor's level the visa standard is equivalent to IELTS 5.5 in each skill. **Lincoln** [summary]: range 6.0-7.0. **Leeds** [summary]: accepts IELTS Academic and for UKVI; rejects results over two years old at course start.
- **King's** (https://www.kcl.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/how-to-apply/english-language-requirements): nationals of a list that includes the USA **who completed a Level 3 qualification in a majority-English-speaking country** need no extra test, except for King's Foundations. This waiver is by nationality plus country of study, so a Taiwanese national studying a US curriculum in Taiwan is **not** covered. English test report numbers must be on the UCAS application; results arrive via King's Apply, not post.
- **Birmingham**: GSAT English at top level can satisfy English for the direct-entry route [summary].
- **St Andrews** page says UKVI "requires students to have passed a SELT" and that its accepted tests may not be accepted by UKVI. This **conflicts** with gov.uk (university can assess B2 itself); it may be stale or may reflect university policy. Pre-sessional courses (10 or 4 weeks) can be an offer condition at St Andrews. https://st-andrews.ac.uk/subjects/entry/taiwan

**Not found**: Duolingo and PTE acceptance at any retrieved university; typical bands for Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, UCL, LSE (only Newcastle 6.5/5.5 and Lincoln 6.0-7.0 were seen); whether a US high school English course waives the test for non-US nationals.

## 4. Money

### 4.1 Visa money [gov]
- Visa fee **GBP 558**; IHS **GBP 776 per year** (see above).
- **Funds**: GBP **1,529 per month** for courses in London (City of London plus the 32 boroughs) or GBP **1,171 per month** outside London, for up to **9 months**. By multiplication: London up to GBP 13,761; elsewhere up to GBP 10,539 (my arithmetic). Plus enough to pay the first year's tuition (the amount on the CAS) minus anything already paid.
- **28-day rule**: funds held for at least 28 days in a row, and the **end date of that period must be within 31 days of the application date** (example on the page: apply 1 January, show the 28 days ending 1 December).
- **Taiwan is on the "differential evidence requirement" list**: you need not submit financial evidence with the application, **but UKVI may ask before deciding**, so the money must still exist and be documented. Source: https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money (page updated 2026-11-03).

### 4.2 Tuition [uni]
- **Cambridge, 2027 entry**, per year, **fixed for the duration of the course**: Group 1 (e.g. History, Law, English, Economics) GBP **30,798**; Mathematics GBP **34,356**; Group 3 (Architecture, Geography, Music) GBP **40,296**; Group 4 (Computer Science, Engineering, Natural Sciences, Management Studies, Psychological and Behavioural Sciences) GBP **46,872**; Medicine / Veterinary GBP **70,554**. Colleges charge a separate fee (amount not read) and ask for financial guarantees. https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/international-students/international-fees-and-costs (page lists 2027 entry; fees for 2028 entry due by 1 Oct 2027)
- **Fee policy differs by university** [summary]: UCL says most overseas UG students get a cohort guarantee (fees fixed over the course) but reserves the right to pass on government-levied charges; Reading raises fees yearly by CPI capped at 4%; Nottingham Trent says fees may rise in later years; Kingston publishes a year-by-year schedule (art and design GBP 21,400 in year 1 to GBP 24,200 by year 4 for 2026/27). Sources: https://www.ucl.ac.uk/study/prospective-students/undergraduate/funding-your-studies , https://www.reading.ac.uk/ready-to-study/study/fees-and-funding/fees-and-funding-ug/international-undergraduate-student-fees , https://www.kingston.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/undergraduate-fees . - Range seen in the sources: roughly GBP 21k (Kingston art and design, year 1) to GBP 47k (Cambridge engineering/CS) for classroom-based UG, GBP 70k medicine. Not a researched market average.

## 6. Pain points and common mistakes

Official-source pain points:
- **Conflicting English-test messaging**: gov.uk says a degree-level university can assess B2 itself; St Andrews tells applicants UKVI requires a SELT. Students over-buy IELTS for UKVI or worry needlessly. [gov vs uni]
- **IELTS General, one-skill retake or an expired result** is rejected (Edinburgh: only Academic versions, single sitting, under two years old on the first day of the intake month). A test taken in December 2025 can expire before a September 2027 start. [uni]
- **Waivers are by nationality**, not by where you study (King's, gov.uk). A Taiwanese student at an American school is not waived by that fact alone. [gov, uni]
- **CAS is valid only 6 months** and some universities issue it only 3-4 months before the start; students book flights/visa slots too early. [uni]
- **Deposits** can be non-refundable GBP 5,000 (Kingston, Brighton) and are required before CAS; many top-ranked universities' UG deposit rules were not published where I looked, so students find out late. [uni]
- **Funds rule**: 28 consecutive days, ending within 31 days of applying; the Taiwan exemption from submitting evidence does not mean UKVI will not ask. [gov]
- **Visa windows**: applying earlier than 6 months before start is not possible; applying inside the UK has a different window (3 months) and longer wait (8 weeks). [gov]
- **Stale pages**: Cambridge's and Oxford's Taiwan entries were flagged 500-870 days old by the search tool; St Andrews still lists SAT Subject Tests; accommodation deadlines are for 2026. A tracker that copies these without an as-of date will mislead. [uni, tool-flagged]
- **ATAS**: easy to miss for integrated-masters undergraduates (30+ working days). [gov]

Counsellor/agent sources: gostudyin.com (https://gostudyin.com/uk/study-in-uk/study-guides/taiwan-student-guide-to-studying-in-the-uk/) says Taiwanese students "typically" need an overall average of about 80%; this is an [agent] snippet that I did not read in full and should not be used as a rule.

No student-forum evidence was gathered (out of budget).

## 7. What a tracker could do

"Generic" means it works from rules on this page; "per-university data" means each university needs researched rows with source URL and as-of date (the same discipline as the site's existing college data).

1. **"Can I apply directly with my diploma?" check** (per-university data).
