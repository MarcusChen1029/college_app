## 2. Systems and steps

### 2.1 OUAC (Ontario universities: U of T, Waterloo, McMaster, Queen's, Western, York, etc.)
What the student does:
1. Create an OUAC account, pick up to N program choices (university + program code), pay once. Current OUAC pages call it "Undergraduate application" with **Group A / Group B** applicants; I could not confirm the older "101 / 105" names in current text. [verified for Group A/B]
2. Application tells you which transcripts/documents you must send (OUAC international page). [verified, page modified 2026-03-27]
3. After submit, each university sends login details for its own applicant portal (U of T: JOINid, Join U of T portal; Engineering Applicant Portal; Waterloo applicant portal; McMaster applicant portal + Kira Talent email within 7-10 business days). The portal holds the real to-do list (supplementary forms, documents, English test, grade updates).
4. Universities decide, mostly in rounds; OUAC only passes data and grades.

Fees (CAD), https://www.ouac.on.ca/guide/undergrad-fees/ [verified; the page prints no year, confirm in the application]:
- Base fee $159 covers the first 3 program choices; each extra choice $51; no fee to swap a choice for another at the same university.
- Per-program add-ons: U of T Engineering $45 (Group A) or $69 (Group B); U of T Rotman Commerce $52; U of T Computer Science (St. George) $52; U of T CS (UTM) $52; U of T Architecture $102; U of T Music $75; Waterloo Engineering $60; Waterloo Architecture $60; McMaster Engineering $50; McMaster Honours Health Sciences $30; York Schulich BBA $50; Guelph Landscape Architecture $75.
- Example arithmetic: U of T Eng + Waterloo Eng + McMaster Eng = 3 choices = $159 + $45/$69 + $60 + $50 = $314 (Group A figure) or $338 (Group B figure).

Grades: for applicants at non-Ontario schools OUAC does not receive grades automatically (the Nov 26 / Feb 18 / May 3 / Jul 15 "grades" dates are for Ontario secondary schools). Grade mechanics seen per university:
- U of T Engineering: student self-reports courses and grades in the ESA "Academic Profile" and keeps updating interim/final grades; uploads transcripts in the portal. [verified]
- Waterloo: transcripts are needed "if you're not currently registered in an Ontario high school or if requested". [verified]
- Queen's: all documents incl. transcripts due Mar 31. [verified]

### 4.2 English tests and waivers
- **UBC (Vancouver, 2027/28 Winter Session)** [verified, https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/english-language-competency/]: nine ways to meet the standard; documents due **Feb 15, 2027**; tests must be taken **on or after Jan 1, 2025**. IELTS Academic 6.5 with no part under 6.0 (IELTS General / Indicator / One Skill Retake not accepted); TOEFL iBT 90 with R22 L22 W21 S21 (new 1-6 scale: overall 4.5, R4.5 L5 W4.5 S4), UBC code 0965; PTE 65 with 60 each skill; Duolingo 125 (115/115/120/120); Cambridge 180; CAEL 70. Waiver routes include four or more consecutive years of full-time education in English in a country where English is the principal language immediately before UBC, four or more years at an eligible international secondary school that teaches in English, IB English A (HL/SL, with grade conditions), plus a request-a-waiver process. An application without proof is **not evaluated** until proof or a waiver arrives.
- U of T: "may need to provide proof through an approved test, unless you qualify for an exemption"; Engineering ESA asks how the requirement will be met; proof due by the document deadline (recommended Dec 1). Test scores/thresholds not opened. [verified wording]
- Waterloo: admissions "may request" scores; due with the document deadline. Thresholds not opened. [verified wording]
- Queen's: English scores due Mar 31, 2027. [verified]
- McGill, Western, McMaster, Alberta: thresholds not opened.

### 4.3 How admission decisions work (what the sources say)
- Program-by-program, competitive/comparative: UBC evaluates against all other applicants to the same degree; looks at all Grade 11 and 12 academic courses, drops the lowest grade (unless a requirement), pays most attention to key subject categories and key courses (e.g. English 12, Pre-Calculus 12 equivalents), then course choices and personal profile. https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/applied/application-evaluation/ [verified]
- Waterloo Engineering: academic performance, AIF and interview weigh most; no school-ranking adjustment described. engineering FAQ [verified]
- Offers before graduation are conditional on final results (U of T international page [verified]; McMaster offers in rounds, non-Ontario offers rolling [search-summary]).
- Waitlists, deferrals: only UBC deferral date (Jul 1, 2027, Vancouver) was verified; others not found.

## 5. Pain points and common mistakes

Label: **O** = implied by an official rule (my inference of the pain), **C** = counselor/student source (none retrieved this session).

1. Same target, different rules per school (O): UBC publishes Taiwan = Senior High School Leaving Certificate with an 84% past average and no GSAT, U of T requires GSAT (or Designated Subject Examination), McGill lists only the certificate. A student cannot tell if GSAT is needed without checking each school.
2. Contradictory or stale pages (O): McGill US page says ACT/SAT required and "test-optional"; Western says SAT 1190/ACT 24 "needed" and "optional for Fall 2027"; Waterloo's "Missing Manual" blurb calls the video interview "optional" while the deadlines/FAQ pages say it is mandatory and the application is denied without it; U of A timeline heading still says Fall 2024; OUAC fee table carries no year.
3. Missing a school-specific supplementary form silently kills the application (O): Waterloo "application will be denied", U of T ESA "will not be considered", UBC "will not be evaluated" without English proof.
4. One-shot, uneditable forms (O): Waterloo AIF can be submitted only once and cannot be viewed afterwards; UBC application cannot be edited after submit; U of T application is locked during review.
5. Date arithmetic is easy to get wrong (O): OUAC Jan 15 is not the Waterloo Engineering finish line (Feb 1), McMaster Engineering is Jan 28, SFU is Jan 31, UBC English is Feb 15, Queen's documents Mar 31; "recommended" dates (Nov 7, Dec 1, Nov 26) drive first-round consideration but are easy to miss.
6. Waterloo Q5 hours window is calendar 2026 (Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2026) while applicants fill it in Nov-Feb (O/inference: students must estimate hours not yet worked).
7. Video formats are stressful and AI-detection is explicit (O): McMaster 10 s prep / 2 min answers; U of T Personal Profile three timed videos; Waterloo online interview; McMaster says its platform monitors suspicious browsing; Waterloo says AI-written or ghost-written answers can cost the offer.
8. Referee logistics (O): UBC wants two referees, one school official for current students, and contacts them only after offers; U of T ESA requires a reference/verification for each extracurricular.
9. Calculus prerequisite trap (O): U of T says Precalculus cannot satisfy the Calculus prerequisite; UBC engineering/science/commerce need "senior-level Math (Pre-Calculus)"; mapping a Taiwanese or international-school math course to these is the student's problem.
10. Notarized, translated documents (O): U of T Engineering asks for original AND notarized English-translated transcripts for Taiwan; UBC accepts photocopies certified by the principal/counsellor but not notarized copies.
11. Counselor/student-forum pain points: not retrieved (budget).
