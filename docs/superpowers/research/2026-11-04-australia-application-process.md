## 2. Routes and steps

### 2a. The three routes
1. **Direct to the university's online portal**: the default for an international student with an overseas qualification (Taiwan senior high, US high school, IB outside Australia/NZ). Melbourne, Sydney, UNSW, ANU, UQ, UWA and Monash all say so. [page]
2. **Through an authorised education agent**: optional almost everywhere, but **mandatory for some countries**:
   - Melbourne: "Applicants who have studied in certain countries are required to apply through an agent to have their documents verified" (country list not on the page). [page]
   - UQ: "Applicants from certain countries must apply through an agent – our online application system will let you know." [page]
   - Monash: applicants located in Crimea, Cuba, Donetsk, Iran, Luhansk, North Korea, Syria or South Sudan must use a registered Monash agent (or IDP). Taiwan is not on this list. [page]
   - Agents can lodge the application, accept the offer and help with the visa (Melbourne, ANU, UQ, UWA say so). Cost to the student: **not found** on any fetched official page. UWA says the non-refundable application fee applies whether the student or an agent applies. [page]
3. **An admissions centre (UAC, VTAC, QTAC, SATAC, TISC)**: required only when currently completing an Australian Year 12 (in or outside Australia) or an IB Diploma **in Australia (or NZ for Melbourne/Monash)**; the official wording differs slightly by university:
   - Sydney: UAC if "an Australian Year 12 qualification in or outside Australia" or "an IB diploma in Australia". [page]
   - Melbourne: VTAC for the start-year intake if Australian Year 12 (in Australia or overseas) or IB in Australia/NZ; "using the incorrect application method may delay or invalidate your application". [page]
   - Monash: VTAC for Australian Year 12, IB in Australia/NZ, or NZ NCEA. [page]
   - ANU: UAC for Australian Senior Secondary Certificate, IB or NCEA Level 3. [page]
   - UQ: QTAC for those completing senior schooling from Australia; everyone else direct. [page]
   - **Implication**: a student at a Taiwanese senior high school, an American school in Taipei, or an IB school in Taiwan does NOT use UAC/VTAC/QTAC. They apply direct (or via an agent).

### 2b. What the student does or produces in a direct application
- Create an account in the university's applicant portal (Melbourne, UQ, UNSW "Applicant Portal"). Rank preferences: Melbourne direct allows **3** courses in one application, assessed in order; "submit only one application at a time, otherwise your assessment will be put on hold". ANU: **3** programs, "we suggest not to list all three preferences with the same entry requirements". [page]
- Documents (Melbourne): transcripts, predicted or final results, English test results if applicable, documents for selection tasks. UQ: certified copy of each transcript (certifiable by an approved UQ agent "or other appropriate parties"). [page]
- Final-year students apply with most recent results. Melbourne: conditional offer, then a "Conditional CoE" option if timing is tight. Predicted results accepted for A-Level, IB and select other curricula under conditions. [page]
- Monash medicine: **no conditional offers**; only final results accepted. [page]

## 3. Entry requirements

### 3a. Taiwan senior high diploma + GSAT (學測): the best-documented university is Melbourne
Source: study.unimelb.edu.au Taiwan entry requirements page. [page] The page gives **no intake year**; scores are labelled "indicative". It offers three ways to qualify: (1) diploma + indicative GPA + indicative GSAT, OR (2) diploma + indicative GPA + SAT/ACT or AP aggregate, OR (3) complete Trinity College Foundation Studies and meet its minimum entry score.

| Melbourne course | Min final GPA | Min GSAT (best 4) | Min SAT or ACT | AP aggregate | Prerequisites |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agriculture | 71 | 33 | 1090 or 20 | 9 (3 to 4 exams) | English, Maths |
| Arts | 81 | 36 | 1120 or 21 | 13 (3 to 4 exams) | English (and Maths for Economics major) |
| Biomedicine | 89 | 44 | 1260 or 26 | 17 (4 exams) | English, Maths, Chemistry |
| Commerce | 90 | 45 | 1270 or 27 | 17 (4 exams) | English, Maths |
| Design | 84 | 38 | 1170 or 23 | 13 (3 to 4 exams) | English (Maths for some majors) |
| Fine Arts | diploma completion | n/a | n/a | n/a | English |
| Music | diploma completion | GSAT taken, no minimum | SAT/ACT taken, no minimum | 3+ AP exams, no minimum | English |
| Oral Health | 95 | 48 | 1330 or 30 | 18 (4 exams only) | English, Biology or Chemistry |
| Science | 86 | 41 | 1210 or 25 | 13 (3 to 4 exams) | English, Maths, one of Bio/Chem/Physics; or English + double Maths |

Rules on the page: GSAT = total of the **best four subjects** (out of five or six sat); **English must be one of the four** for all UG programs; **Maths must be included** for Commerce, Biomedicine, Science. GPA = average of the best four final-year (Year 12) subjects, excluding non-academic subjects. SAT/ACT: composite only, **no superscore**, valid for two years (text is cut after "valid for two yea"). Prerequisites can be met with AP scores of 3+ (AP Calculus AB or BC for Maths; AP Chemistry/Biology/Physics for lab science; AP English Language or Literature for English) or Year 12 Maths A/B or a Calculus and Probability online course (50%+). Double Maths for Science = both AP Calc AB and BC.

**ANU**: [page] study.anu.edu.au/apply/indicative-entry-requirement/taiwan-general-scholastic-ability-test-gsat. GSAT certificate must show 4+ subjects. The required GSAT total depends on the **subject combination group (1 to 6)** and the course's "ANU Entrance Rank". Examples (total needed, Group 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6):
- Rank 80: 38 / 40 / 40 / 40 / 42 / 38
- Rank 90: 45 / 47 / 47 / 47 / 48 / 45
- Rank 95: 49 / 49 / 51 / 52 / 52 / 49
- Rank 99: 55 / 54 / 56 / 57 / 57 / 56
Group 1 includes Chinese+English+Math A+Math B; Group 2 includes Chinese+English+Math A+Social Science; Group 3 Chinese+English+Math A+Natural Science; Group 4 Chinese+English+Math B+Natural Science; Group 5 Chinese+English+Social Science+Natural Science (Group 6 list not read). Which rank each ANU course needs is on the program page (not fetched). Meeting the score does not guarantee entry.

**Sydney**: [page] recognised-qualifications lists "Taiwan General Scholastic Aptitude Test (GSAT)" as accepted. Scores per course are on the course pages (not fetched). [search] says no Sydney GSAT table was found.

**UTS**: [page] "Successful completion of the Taiwan GSAT with results for four or more subjects at an acceptable level, along with evidence of completion of equivalent of year 12". Numeric scores are in the UTS entry-score tables (not read).

**UNSW**: current Taiwan/GSAT figures **not found**. A 2017 UNSW guide (outdated, [3rd-hosted PDF], do not use) described a total of Chinese, English, Maths, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences. UNSW says entry scores are in its "2027 undergraduate entry requirements table" and degree pages. [page]

**Monash (degree)**: Taiwan/GSAT not found. Monash says it accepts SAT (a PDF exists) and "now accepts Gaokao". [page]

**UQ, UWA, Adelaide**: Taiwan requirement pages not found.

**Monash College Foundation Year** (a pathway into Monash; the page is under the "2025" data folder, so 2025 data): [page] Taiwan Senior High School Diploma: overall average of Grade 10, 11 and 12 subjects (including fails), a minimum total of **150 credits**, **60%** (Standard and Extended). GSAT: total of the best 4 subjects **excluding Chinese**, minimum **6 in each subject**, total **24**.

## 5. Offers and pathways

- **Conditional offers**: Melbourne and ANU issue them while final results, English evidence or documents are pending; Melbourne's offer must be made unconditional before enrolment; a "Conditional CoE" lets visa work start early. [page] Monash medicine issues none. [page]
- **Guaranteed entry scores**: Melbourne publishes "guaranteed" and "minimum" scores for some qualifications (A-Levels, IB, foundation): at or above the guaranteed score with prerequisites and English met, the offer "is assured"; below it, selection depends on ranking. Guaranteed scores apply only if no further study since school or foundation. [page]
- **Package offers**:
  - UQ: English-language course at UQ College + a conditional UQ offer. Tick "package offer" when applying to UQ; do not apply to UQ College separately. Pathways: Bridging English (10 to 30 weeks, final assessment satisfies UQ English entry) or Integrated English (5 to 45 weeks, then take an approved test). [page]
  - Monash: Monash University Foundation Year (MUFY) "packaged offer" holders get a conditional packaged offer to a Monash degree; MUFY finishing Nov 2026 leads to Feb/March 2027 start. [page]
  - Melbourne: Trinity College Foundation Studies (about 700 international students a year) has guaranteed pathways to Melbourne; University of Melbourne English Language Bridging Program (UMELBP, 10 weeks) can satisfy English. Graduate Degree Packages combine UG + graduate place for high scorers. [page]
- **Foundation / diploma**: Trinity (dates in 1e); Monash College Foundation Year (Taiwan entry values in 3a); UNSW College (Term 1 2027 diploma offers in Dec and Jan, applied for directly at the UNSW College site) [search]. UNSW College entry values, Taylors and Monash College diploma not read.
- **Credit for AP/IB**: Monash credit page exists; AP/IB credit rules not researched.
- **Offer validity/timing**: UNSW rounds give an explicit acceptance deadline (section 1c). Melbourne gives instructions in the offer. Other universities: not read.

## 7. Pain points and common mistakes

Official-source warnings (type: university page):
1. Using the wrong application route (admissions centre vs direct) "may delay or invalidate" the application (Melbourne).
2. Submitting several separate direct applications instead of one ranked application puts assessment on hold (Melbourne).
3. UNSW quota can fill before the published deadline (Commerce, Medicine); late applications are only considered if the faculty's quota is not full.
4. TOEFL: new format and MyBest not accepted at UNSW; Home Edition not accepted at Sydney. Test validity is two years at commencement (Sydney, UNSW, UQ).
5. SAT/ACT: no superscoring at Melbourne; valid two years.
6. Medicine: no conditional offers at Monash; final certified results arriving in January 2027 may miss the offer round.
7. Unlike the US Common App, one application can list only 3 preferences (Melbourne, ANU), so choose course tiers deliberately; ANU advises against listing three courses with the same entry bar.
8. UWA does not process an application until the fee has charged.

Source-reliability warning (type: search-result observation): a third-party site gave UWA S1 2027 as 28 Dec 2026 while UWA's own page says 11 Jan 2027; a third-party guide gave Sydney as "a specific day in January"; Sydney's own page says 1 Dec 2026. Third-party sites are frequently stale, so the tracker should store only official dates with a "verified on" date.

Student/counselor pain-point sources (forums, counselors): **not searched in this pass**; no claims made.
