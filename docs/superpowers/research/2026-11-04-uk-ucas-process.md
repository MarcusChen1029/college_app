Researched 2026-11-04 for the product brainstorm on UK support. Scope: the UCAS undergraduate application itself, from opening an account to confirmation.
Out of scope (another agent): qualification recognition (AP / IB / Taiwan), English tests, visas / CAS, tuition fees, scholarships.

How to read the evidence tags used below:
- **[official-scraped]** page text read directly (UCAS pages fetched through Firecrawl before its credits ran out).

- **[secondary]** a university clearing page or a parent-guide blog. Never used for a rule, only flagged where no official 2027 page was found.
- Nothing here is from memory. "Not found" means I looked and could not confirm.

## 2. Steps: what the student does, who else is involved, what comes back

Each step: STUDENT does / OTHERS involved / COMES BACK.

1. **Register in UCAS Hub** (open since 12 May 2026). STUDENT enters basic details and security information, about five minutes. If applying through a school, the school gives a **buzzword** that links the application to it. Independent applicants answer "No" to "applying through a school or college" and go straight into the form (UCAS blog, date unstated, **[official-summary]** https://www.ucas.com/connect/blogs/how-to-apply-independent-applicant). Source for Hub and buzzword: https://www.ucas.com/discover/advice-for-parents-carers-and-guardians/how-to-fill-in-the-ucas-application **[official-scraped]**.
2. **Fill in the form** (save and return any time). Sections listed by UCAS: Personal details (incl. student-support questions); Contact details (incl. optional "nominated access" for a parent); Where you live (last three years and purpose); Supporting information (EU / EEA / Switzerland links); **English language skills** (IELTS / TOEFL details, which UCAS says are **not passed on to the choices**); Finance and funding; Diversity and inclusion; More about you (refreshed for 2027: conditions, disabilities, learning differences); **Education** (every school since age 11, results, pending exams); Employment; Extra activities (UK applicants only); **Personal statement**; then **Reference** (added by the referee). New for 2027: students may **upload supporting documents** into relevant sections (optional; UK students in formal education are not expected to upload UK qualification documents). **[official-scraped]** same page; 2027 changes from https://www.ucas.com/advisers/help-and-training/toolkits/2027-cycle-toolkit **[official-scraped]**.
3. **Choose up to five courses.** The fee covers up to five choices (six for conservatoires). Choices can be the same or different courses at the same or different universities. For medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine and veterinary science, **at most four courses in any one of those subjects** (UCAS filling-in page excerpt, **[official-summary]**; no separate 2027-specific page found, but it is the standing UCAS rule). Later-deadline choices can be added after 15 Oct, still capped at five.
4. **Write the personal statement** (see section 3 and the format below).
5. **Referee writes the reference, predicted grades are added** (see section 4). OTHERS: school teacher / counsellor, or independent referee. A student **cannot submit until the reference is on the application**.
6. **Pay and send.** School route: pressing Send sends it to the school; the school checks it, writes the reference, pays and sends to UCAS, usually with an internal deadline earlier than UCAS's. Independent route: student pays once the reference is in. **Fee for 2027 entry: GBP 34.50 for up to five choices**; waivers exist for UK free-school-meals and care-leaver applicants (UK-only). The application can be cancelled within 14 days of sending for a full refund. **[official-scraped]** parent page above; refund line **[official-summary]** from https://www.ucas.com/discover/advice-for-parents-carers-and-guardians/what-happens-once-ucas-gets-an-application.
7. **Pre-application tests and university-specific forms** (section 3): UCAT, LNAT, ESAT, TMUA, TARA; My Cambridge Application; Oxford written work.
8. **Interviews** where used (Oxford, Cambridge, medicine; Imperial departments; LSE does not interview).
9. **Decisions arrive in UCAS Hub** as: conditional offer (needs results, usually exams), unconditional offer (place is yours; if firm, binding regardless of grades), unsuccessful, or withdrawn. **[official-summary]** parent page above.
10. **Reply**: choose one **Firm** and one **Insurance** (insurance applies only if firm conditions are missed, so usually lower). Everything else is declined. After replying there are **14 days to change replies, once only** (same page). Missing a reply deadline means **all offers are declined by default** (section 1 dates).
11. **If no offers or all declined**: **Extra** (25 Feb to 1 Jul), then Clearing.
12. **Results day**: students check Hub for place confirmation; if conditions are missed or beaten, Clearing; **self-release** into Clearing from 2 Jul onward is an official feature (key-dates page); the detailed Adjustment mechanism was **not found** in this pass.

### The new personal statement (current format, confirmed for the 2026 cycle; 2027 change not seen)
Source: https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/writing-your-personal-statement/how-to-write-your-personal-statement **[official-scraped]**, a live page whose own anchors still mention "2026 entry". The 2027 toolkit's "what's new" list does not mention a personal statement change, but that is absence of evidence, not confirmation.
- Three questions, one answer box each, **4,000 characters total including spaces**, **minimum 350 characters per answer** (labelled on the boxes, with an overall counter).
  1. Why do you want to study this course or subject?
  2. How have your qualifications and studies helped you to prepare for this course or subject?
  3. What else have you done to prepare outside of education, and why are these experiences useful?
- Admissions staff read it as a whole; split between answers is free; there is no "wrong" box for evidence. (Whether one statement goes to every choice was not stated on the pages I read; UCAS says this only for the reference.)
- UCAS has an AI / ChatGPT guidance page and a Hub "personal statement builder".

## 3. Variations that matter

### Oxford (all **[official-summary]** from ox.ac.uk search excerpts; ox.ac.uk blocks fetchers)
- UCAS deadline 15 Oct 2026 18:00. UAT-UK tests (ESAT, TMUA, TARA) must be booked by **28 Sep 2026 18:00** and sat 12-16 Oct, in the **October** sitting (Oxford generally requires October; Astrophoria Foundation Year may use Jan 4-8 with booking to 21 Dec). Results via the candidate's UAT-UK account on **16 Nov 2026**.
- **Written work** for some courses: arrive at college by **10 Nov 2026**, max 2,000 words per piece; Fine Art portfolio earlier.
- **Interviews online in December 2026** (Magdalen: 7-18 Dec); cannot be rearranged, so the student must be free throughout.
- Not found: the per-course test map (which Oxford course needs which test). Oxford's excerpt only says LNAT and UCAT are for Law and Medicine. Needs the Oxford "summary table of admissions requirements" page, which I could not fetch.

### Cambridge (**[official-summary]** from https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/applying/admission-assessments and /apply/how/cambridge-application, both labelled 2027 entry)
- Second form: **My Cambridge Application** (formerly the SAQ), emailed within 48 h of the UCAS submission, due **22 Oct 2026 18:00** for most applicants. Contents mentioned: school subjects, English test scores and dates, SAT scores if required, an **optional Cambridge-specific personal statement up to 1,200 characters**, photo, **transcript** ("can take several weeks, request early"), funding details for international applicants, and a **GBP 60 application fee for applicants from outside the UK / Ireland / Channel Islands / Isle of Man** (some exempt). For 2027 entry Cambridge will **not** download or review documents uploaded into UCAS; documents go through MCA or the College.
- Pre-interview tests to register for: **ESAT** (Chemical Eng and Biotech, Engineering, Natural Sciences, Veterinary Medicine), **TMUA** (Computer Science, Economics, Mathematics), **UCAT** (Medicine A100 and Graduate), **LNAT** (Law). Computer Science at Peterhouse / Trinity also needs the CSAT (external site). College-arranged assessments after an interview invitation (no registration) for Architecture, Classics, English, Geography, History (and joint), HSPS, Linguistics, MML, Music, Philosophy, PBS, Design, Asian and Middle Eastern Studies. No assessment: Anglo-Saxon Norse and Celtic, Archaeology, Education, Environment Law and Economics, History of Art, Theology. Not mentioned on that page: TARA, Cambridge Law Test, registration dates.
- Interviews: December (Jesus College: first three weeks of Dec 2026). Applicants outside the UK interviewed online (Trinity Hall). Christ's College says international and UK applicants share the same deadline.
- Cambridge Vet Med uses ESAT, a detail a Taiwan student would not guess.

### Medicine / dentistry / veterinary
- 15 Oct deadline for most courses (check each course in UCAS search); **UCAT** must be sat in the summer before applying (13 Jul to 24 Sep 2026; fee GBP 70 UK / GBP 115 outside the UK; bursaries exist; UCAT sends the result to the chosen UK universities in early November). Only one sitting per cycle. Maximum four choices in one of the subjects. MMI format interviews: **not researched** (no source fetched).
- 2027-cycle UCAT dates (for 2028 entry) not yet published; ucat.ac.uk says dates by Feb 2027, booking instructions March 2027. **[official-summary]** https://www.ucat.ac.uk/about-ucat/ucat-test-dates/

### LSE (**[official-summary]** lse.ac.uk search summary)
- Deadline 13 Jan 2027 18:00 for equal consideration; early applications are held in a "gathered field" so applying early gives no advantage. International applicants "strongly advised" to apply by 13 Jan because places after the deadline are rarely available. Decisions on on-time applications by 12 May 2027.
- Tests: **LNAT** for LLB; **TMUA** required for Economics-department programmes, recommended for Maths and Statistics; some applicants invited to a UGAA. **No interviews**, so the personal statement carries more weight.
- English proof is not required at application; asked after offer if needed.

### Imperial (**[official-summary]** imperial.ac.uk search summary; its Deadlines page has a heading "2026 entry" above 2027 dates, so re-check)
- UCAS deadline 13 Jan 2027 18:00; **no UCAS Extra and no Clearing**. ESAT (Aeronautics, Chemical Eng, Civil, Design Eng, EEE, Life Sciences, Mechanical, Physics) and TMUA (Mathematics, Economics-Finance-Data Science) both have **two windows**: 12-16 Oct 2026 and 4-8 Jan 2027 (China / HK / Macau use different days); no advantage to either window. **All interviews online** (Engineering 20-25 min with Teams; Chemistry about 20 min; Economics 20-30 min); Mathematics normally has no interview.
- UCAT not seen for Imperial in these results (not verified).

### UCL, KCL
- LNAT sit-by date of 31 Dec 2026 is the only fact obtained (LNAT page). Course-level test and interview rules not researched.

### Other tests named in scope
- ESAT, TMUA, TARA (UAT-UK; Pearson VUE; Taiwan test-centre availability **not confirmed**), UCAT, LNAT: covered above. TARA appears in the Oxford excerpt only. For UAT-UK tests, results appear in the candidate's UAT-UK account on 16 Nov 2026 and Oxford receives them after that (Oxford excerpt); for UCAT, results go to chosen UK universities in early November (ucat.ac.uk excerpt).

## 4. Applicants without a UK school (Taiwan senior high, US-style / international school, IB)

What the official pages say:
- **Two routes.** (a) Through a school / college / centre: the school must be a UCAS **registered centre** to issue a buzzword; the school reads the application, adds the reference and predicted grades, pays and sends. (b) **Independent**: choose "not through a school", name a referee by email in the reference section. https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-university/how-get-ucas-reference **[official-scraped]**.
- **Independent referee rules** (https://www.ucas.com/advisers/help-and-training/guides-resources-and-training/writing-references/writing-undergraduate-references-for-independent-applicants **[official-scraped]**): someone who knows the applicant academically or professionally, **never family, friend, partner or ex-partner, and not the applicant**; "if you do your application may be cancelled" (student page). The referee cannot see the application, so the student must tell them which courses, and the referee must **avoid naming any university**. Written **in English**, online, in **one session** (time-out after 35 minutes of inactivity, no draft saving), **max 3,800 characters**, no bold / italics / underline, cannot be edited after submit. Referee signs in at the 2027 independent-referee portal (https://2027.undergrad.applyreference.ucas.com/indepref/LoginServlet) with a password emailed by UCAS. A student can swap referees with "Ask a different person to write reference". A standard HR employment reference is "generally not accepted".
- **Reference structure** (independent guidance): Section 1 mandatory overview of relationship; Section 2 extenuating circumstances (tick "no information" if none); Section 3 other supportive information relevant to the courses, short factual statements. For centre-linked applications there is the same three-section structure plus a Section 1 statement about the school and its predicted-grade process (centre guidance page https://www.ucas.com/advisers/help-and-training/guides-resources-and-training/writing-references/ucas-registered-centre-linked-applications-undergraduate-references, **[official-summary]** excerpt only).
- **Agents and counsellors** can be registered centres, but UCAS reminds them the reference is academic and linked to predicted grades, so it needs someone who knows the applicant's academic achievements. (centre guidance excerpt above).
- **Linking later**: an independent applicant can ask an old teacher for the school's buzzword and link afterwards, the school then formally accepts the request. (student reference page, **[official-scraped]**). Reference processes page title confirms "Independent applicants wanting to link to a centre" exists (**[official-summary]**).
- **Predicted grades** (https://www.ucas.com/advisers/help-and-training/guides-resources-and-training/application-overview/predicted-grades-what-you-need-know-entry-year **[official-scraped]**): the grade the school believes the student will likely achieve in positive circumstances; required for **all pending qualifications** unless the qualification's structure makes it impossible; set independent of the university choices; **finalised by the point of submission** (highly selective courses may not fix later data-entry errors). If the referee cannot give them, they tick **"I am unable to provide any predicted grades"** and the applicant is emailed. If the applicant has no pending qualifications, none are asked. Where the referee lacks in-depth knowledge, UCAS says to explain in Section 2 of the reference.
- **Not found**: any official rule on how a Taiwanese school should express GSAT / 學測 or a US school should express AP / SAT as UCAS "predicted grades"; what each target university accepts in place of A-level-style predictions. That is per-university, and also overlaps the other agent's qualification scope.

Practical consequences for the product (inference, not UCAS statements): a Taiwan senior-high student likely needs to know on day one whether their school is a registered centre; if not, the plan must include finding an academic referee who writes English and will do it in one 35-minute session; IB schools more often have a predictions process that maps directly.

## 5. Pain points and common mistakes

Honest labelling: I did not collect counselor or forum sources in this pass (credits ran out), so everything below is either an official warning or my own inference from the rules. Treat as hypotheses to confirm with Taiwanese counselors.

| Pain point | Type |
|---|---|
| Student cannot submit until the reference is attached; with a school this adds the school's internal deadline, with an independent referee it depends on a third party | Official rule (UCAS reference page) |
| UCAS tells students to follow the **school's internal deadline**, not the UCAS one | Official (deadlines page) |
| Independent referee loses work after 35 min idle; cannot amend after submit; 3,800-char cap | Official (referee guidance) |
| Tests must be **booked and sat before the UCAS deadline**: UCAT booking closes 16 Sep, UAT-UK booking 28 Sep, LNAT Oxbridge booking by 15 Sep. A student who starts in October has already missed the Oxbridge / medicine route for 2027 | Official dates, my inference on consequence |
| January 2027 date is **13 Jan**, flagged by UCAS as a change for this cycle; any dated guide copied from earlier years will be a day off | Official (2027 toolkit "key date changes") |
| Extra is for applicants with five choices and **no offers**; RBD / DBD dates are different per applicant (DBD triggers by when the last decision lands). Missing a reply deadline declines **all** offers | Official |
| Imperial takes no Extra or Clearing; LSE rarely has places after 13 Jan, so "apply late and use Clearing" is not a fallback for those | Official (university pages, summary) |
| Cambridge needs a **second form one week after UCAS** (22 Oct), a transcript that takes weeks, and for overseas applicants a GBP 60 fee; easy to miss because it is outside UCAS | Official (Cambridge page, summary) |
| UCAS's English-language section is **not passed to the choices**, so students might think their IELTS is "uploaded" when it is not | Official (UCAS parent page); the "might think" is inference |
| Oxford written work due 10 Nov and interviews in December; both inside the weeks after the 15 Oct deadline when students are also applying elsewhere | Official dates, inference |
| Mixed-subject choice sets (e.g. Economics and Law) make one personal statement hard to write; UCAS says the reference goes to every choice, so it must not name a university | Inference, plus official rule for the reference |
| Per-course test requirements differ for the same university (Imperial, Cambridge, LSE) | Official (university pages) |

## 6. What a tracker could do

Tag: **[G]** generic (same for every UK university; build once). **[U]** needs per-university data.

Data model ideas
1. **[G] UCAS application tracker card** per student: stage (Hub registered / form started / reference requested / ref done / predicted grades in / fee paid / sent / offers in / replied / results), route (school registered centre vs independent), buzzword (stored as plain text label), referee name and status.
2. **[G] Choice-limit checker**: max 5 choices; max 4 in medicine / dentistry / veterinary medicine / veterinary science; show remaining slots and which choices fall under which deadline (15 Oct vs 13 Jan). Needs a deadline-group flag on UK course rows (**[U]** to fill in: Oxbridge + most medicine / dentistry / vet = 15 Oct; most others = 13 Jan; some courses differ, UCAS says check each course in search).
3. **[G] Dated reminder pack for 2027 entry** generated from section 1 (Hub opens, 1 Sep send, 15 Oct, 13 Jan, 25 Feb Extra, reply DBD dates, 30 Jun, 2 Jul, 22 Jul, 23 Sep, 18 Oct). Allow a **school-internal deadline** field that overrides the displayed UCAS date, mirroring UCAS's own Hub behaviour.
4. **[G] Reply-decision helper** after offers: firm and insurance pickers, a check that insurance has less demanding conditions, a "14 days / one change" clock, DBD countdown (the personal date depends on when the last decision arrives: store decision dates and compute the reply date by the 31 Mar / 12 May / 14 Jul rule).
5. **[G] Personal statement workspace**: three boxes, 350-char minimum per box, 4,000-char shared counter with spaces included, warn if any box is under 350, and **paste-from-Google-Docs counts spaces and line breaks the way UCAS counts them (test this, not verified)**. Link into the existing Essays area.
6. **[G] Reference brief for the referee** (a one-page export, EN + zh-TW): list of the five chosen courses (without naming universities in the text), reminders: English only, 3,800 chars, one session, no formatting, predicted grades for every pending qualification, three section headings. Plus a prompt list for Section 2 and 3.
7. **[G] Predicted-grades worksheet**: list pending qualifications, ask school for a grade per qualification, flag "unable to provide" case and show what to tell universities.
8. **[U] Admissions-test planner** per university and per course: required test, registration open / close, sitting window, results date, mandatory-before-application flag. Seeded from the sources in section 7 (UCAT 13 Jul to 24 Sep; LNAT dates above; ESAT / TMUA 12-16 Oct and 4-8 Jan; TARA). Re-check each year; UCAT 2027 dates arrive Feb 2027.
9. **[U] "Second form" tracker**: My Cambridge Application (22 Oct, transcript, GBP 60 fee outside UK), Oxford written work (10 Nov), interview window (Dec), portfolio flags, CSAT for CS at Peterhouse / Trinity.
10. **[U] Per-university application-features flags**: participates in Extra? Clearing? interviews? written work? course-specific personal-statement guidance? (Imperial: no Extra / Clearing; LSE: no interviews). This directly feeds the choice-mix advice.
11. **[G] Eligibility sanity box**: "Is your school a UCAS registered centre?" with a decision tree to the independent-referee route; link to UCAS referee rules in plain language (zh-TW).
12. **[G] Timezone helper**: all UK deadlines at 18:00 UK time (GMT / BST) converted to Taiwan time (UTC+8). The conversion must handle UK clock changes (GMT in winter, BST in summer); use a tz library, do not hard-code an offset. Valuable because "18:00" in Taipei is a very different moment.
13. **[G] Status import**: UCAS Hub has no public API found in this pass; manual status entry only. (Not researched, so treat as "unknown".)
14. **[G] Results-day panel**: results-day date (A-level 12 Aug 2027 is secondary-sourced, verify when UCAS publishes), Clearing and self-release explainer, Extra rules, last dates (1 Jul, 22 Jul, 23 Sep, 18 Oct).

Warnings to bake into the UI:
- Show every date with its **cycle label** ("2027 entry") and source URL, since UCAS and university pages mix cycles (LSE, Imperial, Oxford pages each had stale headings).
- Because today is already past 15 Oct, the default 2027 view for new users should lead with the 13 Jan route and what is still open: LNAT for KCL / LSE / UCL (sit by 31 Dec), ESAT / TMUA Jan window (4-8 Jan; booking closes 21 Dec per Oxford Foundation Year page), UCAS reference lead time.
